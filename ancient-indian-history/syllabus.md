# Ancient Indian History

- Prehistoric Cultures: Stone ages (Paleolithic, Mesolithic, Neolithic) and Chalcolithic/Megalithic phases.
- Indus Valley Civilization (IVC): Origins, mature phase (extent, society, economy, culture), foreign contacts, and factors leading to decline.
- Vedic Period: Rigvedic and later Vedic societies, Vedic literature, religion, Upanishads, political/social structures, and the evolution of the Varna system and monarchy.
- Religious Movements: Rise of Buddhism and Jainism (causes for spread, teachings, councils, and texts).
- Mahajanapadas & Urbanization: Formation of states from Mahajanapadas to the Nanda Empire.
- Mauryan Empire: Chandragupta Maurya, Megasthenes, Ashoka's inscriptions and Dhamma, administration, and art.
- Post-Mauryan India (c. 200 BC – AD 300): Shungas, Kanvas, Satavahanas, Indo-Greeks, Shakas, Kushanas, and evolution of Jatis.
- Gupta and Vakataka Age: Political history, administration, golden age achievements in science and art.
- Post-Gupta / Early Medieval Dynasties: Harshavardhana, Pallavas, Chalukyas, Cholas, and regional kingdoms of South India.
