# Medieval Indian History

- Early Medieval India: Rise of regional dynasties and the Tripartite Struggle.
- Delhi Sultanate (13th to 16th Century): Mamluk, Khalji, Tughlaq, Sayyid, and Lodi dynasties, administration, economy, and architecture.
- Vijayanagara and Bahmani Kingdoms: Political developments, administration, society, and trade.
- Bhakti and Sufi Movements: Major saints, teachings, and their impact on society and culture.
- Mughal Empire: Babur, Humayun, Sher Shah Suri, Akbar, Jahangir, Shah Jahan, Aurangzeb, administrative policies, land revenue systems, and religious/ Rajput policies.
- Marathas and Sikhs: Rise of Shivaji, Peshwa administration, and the evolution of the Sikh community and Khalsa Panth.
- Advent of Europeans: Arrival of Portuguese, Dutch, French, and English trading companies.
