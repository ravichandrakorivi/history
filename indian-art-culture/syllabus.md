# Indian Art & Cultture

- Architecture and Sculpture: Harappan, Mauryan, Gupta, Nagara, Dravidian, and Vesara temple architecture.
- Paintings: Mural paintings (Ajanta, Sittanavasal) and miniature painting schools (Mughal, Deccan, Rajput, Pahari).
- Performing Arts: Classical and folk dances, Indian music styles (Hindustani and Carnatic), and traditional theatre and puppetry.
- Literature and Philosophy: Classical languages, literature, and the six schools of Indian philosophy.
