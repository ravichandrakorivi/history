# Modern Indian History

- Expansion and Consolidation of British Power: Carnatic Wars, conquest of Bengal (Plassey and Buxar), Mysore, Maratha, and Sikh wars.
- British Policies & Economic Impact: Land revenue systems (Permanent Settlement, Ryotwari, Mahalwari), deindustrialization, and the drain of wealth theory.
- Revolt of 1857: Causes, spread, key leaders, failure, and immediate aftermath (Crown rule).
- Socio-Religious Reform Movements: Raja Ram Mohan Roy, Dayananda Saraswati, Jyotirao Phule, and reforms concerning sati, widow remarriage, and caste.
- Tribal and Peasant Uprisings: Santhal, Indigo, Munda, and other regional rebellions.
- Indian National Movement (1885–1947):
  - Moderate and Extremist phases of the Indian National Congress.
  - Gandhian era (Non-Cooperation, Civil Disobedience, and Quit India movements).
  - Revolutionary nationalism, constitutional developments, and the role of various leaders.
  - Partition, independence, and the integration of princely states.
